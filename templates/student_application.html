Application Guidelines

  • You must be a currently enrolled full-time student to apply for hostel accommodation.
  • Keep your admission/registration number, government ID, and a recent passport-size photo ready before starting the form.
  • Applications are processed on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to eligibility and seat availability.
  • You may select up to 3 room/hostel preferences in order of priority.
  • Incomplete or incorrect applications may be rejected during document verification.
  • You will be notified of your application status and allotment on this dashboard once processed.
  • Hostel fees must be paid within the deadline mentioned in your allotment notice, or your seat may be released.
